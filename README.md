## 🚀 About Us

**Glober GTI Lab** is a research lab focused on developing AI-powered career intelligence systems. Our mission is to democratize access to high-quality career guidance through advanced AI technologies.

We specialize in:
- 🤖 **Multi-Agent AI Systems** — Orchestrating specialized agents for complex career consulting tasks
- 🔍 **Evidence-Driven Research** — Real-time web research with source tracking and verification
- 🧠 **Case-Based Reasoning (CBR)** — Learning from successful career trajectories
- 🌐 **Bilingual Intelligence** — Professional Chinese-English report generation

## 🎯 Our Products

### Glober Career Assistant

An end-to-end AI career consulting platform that guides users from profile building to offer acceptance.

**Key Features:**
- ✅ Smart Profiling — Dialogue-based user profiling with adaptive questioning
- ✅ Deep Research — Real-time web research with evidence tracking
- ✅ Strategy Generation — AI-powered career strategy with action plans
- ✅ Bilingual Reports — Professional 14-chapter career reports in Chinese & English
- ✅ Task Coaching — Continuous guidance through the job search process
- ✅ Mock Interviews — AI-powered interview preparation
